[B][SIZE=3]Why another list library?[/SIZE][/B]
Creating multi‑column scroll lists in the Elder Scrolls Online UI is verbose and repetitive. [COLOR=#d4af37]LibScrollList[/COLOR] wraps the native [I]ZO_ScrollList[/I] into a clean, column‑based API with built‑in sorting, headers, and reusable cell types. Spend less time wrestling with anchors and more time building your UI.

[B][SIZE=3]Highlights[/SIZE][/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Column‑first design[/B] – define columns with labels, textures, or buttons; the library handles layout.
[*][B]Automatic sorting[/B] – click column headers to sort ascending/descending, with optional multi‑column sorting.
[*][B]Flexible cell types[/B] – ready‑to‑use Label, Texture, and Button cells; add your own with simple OOP.
[*][B]Clean, [COLOR="deepskyblue"]documented API[/COLOR][/B] – method names that make sense, plus a full [COLOR="deepskyblue"]README with examples on GitHub[/COLOR].
[*][B]Lightweight[/B] – no extra dependencies, just pure Lua that respects ESO’s UI conventions.
[/LIST]
